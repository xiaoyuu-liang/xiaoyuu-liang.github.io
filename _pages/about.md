I am currently a MPhil/PhD student at [University College London](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/), advised by [Prof. Lorenzo Cavallaro](https://s2lab.cs.ucl.ac.uk/people/sullivan/) at the [Systems Security Research Lab (S2Lab)](https://s2lab.cs.ucl.ac.uk). Before joining UCL, I received my Master's degree in Cyberspace Security and Bachelor's degree in Information Security from [Beihang University](https://ev.buaa.edu.cn/), under the supervision of [Dr. Haohua Du](https://cst.buaa.edu.cn/info/1113/2720.htm) at Beihang University. 

My research focuses on **Trustworthy AI**, including topics such as uncertainty and robustness.
