I am an MPhil/PhD student at [University College London](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/), advised by [Prof. Lorenzo Cavallaro](https://s2lab.cs.ucl.ac.uk/people/sullivan/) at the [Systems Security Research Lab (S2Lab)](https://s2lab.cs.ucl.ac.uk). Before joining UCL, I received my Master's degree in Cyberspace Security and my Bachelor's degree in Information Security from [Beihang University](https://ev.buaa.edu.cn/), where I was supervised by [Dr. Haohua Du](https://cst.buaa.edu.cn/info/1113/2720.htm).

My research focuses on **Trustworthy AI**. At UCL I work on **uncertainty quantification for large language models**, using mechanistic interpretability to understand where uncertainty is observed inside a model. Before that, I worked on **certified robustness** for black-box graph neural networks.
